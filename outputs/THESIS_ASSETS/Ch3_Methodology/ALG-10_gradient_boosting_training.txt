Algorithm
  1  GB <- GradientBoostingClassifier(n_estimators 200, learning_rate 0.1, max_depth 3, subsample 1, min_samples_leaf 1) (classic implementation)
  2  M5 <- ClassWeightedClassifier(GB, class_weight balanced)
  3  fit(X, y):
  4      w_i <- targets.size / (classes.size * counts.astype(float)), evaluated at y_i's class (balanced weighting)
  5      if sample weights are supplied: w <- w x supplied
  6      F_0 <- log-odds of the weighted class prior
  7      for each boosting stage m: fit a regression tree of depth <= 3 to the weighted negative gradient on a 1 fraction of rows; F_m <- F_(m-1) + 0.1 x tree
  8  predict_proba <- sigmoid(F) for two classes, softmax over per-class F otherwise
  9  calibrated inside the ensemble: true

Notes
