Algorithm ALG-08: SVM training and probability calibration
PV-MEPCG / PulseVision  |  T98.4  |  objective O3

Purpose: M3: an RBF support vector machine whose scores are turned into probabilities by an explicit, visible calibration fitted inside the training fold.
Input:   training rows X, y (optionally their subject groups)
Output:  calibrated class probabilities whose argmax is the prediction

Algorithm
  1  svc <- SVC(kernel rbf, C 1, gamma scale, class_weight balanced); probability is never enabled
  2  M3 <- CalibratedSVM(svc, method sigmoid, cv 3, ensemble true)
  3  fit(X, y):
  4      refuse an inner estimator with probability enabled; forward class_weight to the inner SVC
  5      if method is isotonic and |y| < 1000: warn that it overfits
  6      split the training rows into 3 calibration folds, subject-grouped when a grouped split is supplied
  7      for each calibration fold: fit svc on its training part; fit the sigmoid p = 1 / (1 + exp(A f(x) + B)) to svc's decision values on its held part
  8      keep every (svc, sigmoid) pair; predict_proba <- their mean probability
  9  predict <- argmax of predict_proba, so the two can never disagree

Notes
  Searched space: C log-uniform [0.01, 1000], gamma log-uniform [0.0001, 10], class_weight {balanced, none}.
