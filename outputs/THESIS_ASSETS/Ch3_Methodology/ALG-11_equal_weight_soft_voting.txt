Algorithm ALG-11: Equal-weight soft voting
PV-MEPCG / PulseVision  |  T99.1  |  objective O3

Purpose: M6, the mandatory baseline ensemble: the members' calibrated probabilities are averaged with equal weights, and the binary decision threshold is chosen inside the training fold rather than fixed.
Input:   training rows X, y and the training fold's own subject groups
Output:  fused class probabilities, and for binary tasks the in-fold threshold t*

Algorithm
   1  members <- M3, M4, M5, built from config; members in M5 are wrapped in the explicit sigmoid calibration of ALG-08
   2  M6 <- SoftVotingEnsemble(members, weights equal, inner_cv 3, objective balanced_accuracy, tune_threshold true, seed 42); groups are the training fold's, never the full matrix's
   3  fit(X, y): refuse anything but soft voting
   4      w <- (1/m, ..., 1/m) over the m members
   5      if the task is binary and tune_threshold:
   6          inner <- 3 stratified splits of the training rows, subject-grouped when groups are supplied
   7          for each member and each inner split (T, V): fit a fresh clone on T; oof[member, V] <- its predict_proba on V
   8          reorder every member's columns to the ensemble's class order; refuse a member that never saw a class; assert every training row got a probability
   9          p_oof <- fuse(oof, w) = np.tensordot(vector / total, stack, axes=(0, 0))
  10          candidates <- midpoints between adjacent distinct scores, (unique[:-1] + unique[1:]) / 2.0 (quantiles when more than 200), plus the fixed reference: np.unique(np.concatenate([candidates, [0.5]]))
  11          t* <- the candidate maximising balanced_accuracy = 0.5 x sensitivity + 0.5 x specificity; ties to the cut-off nearest the fixed reference, key -abs(float(threshold) - 0.5); the fixed-reference metrics are recorded beside it
  12  refit every member on the whole training fold
  13  predict_proba(X) <- fuse(member probabilities on X, w)
  14  predict(X) <- positive class where p(positive) >= t* for binary tasks; argmax of the fused vector otherwise

Notes
  The outer test fold is never scored while w or t* is chosen: both come from out-of-fold probabilities over inner splits of the training rows.
