Algorithm
  1  RF <- RandomForestClassifier(n_estimators 500, max_depth none, min_samples_leaf 1, max_features sqrt, random_state 42)
  2  class_weight <- balanced, set inside the fold pipeline from the training labels
  3  for each tree t: draw a bootstrap sample of the training rows; grow the tree, choosing each split among sqrt random candidate features by weighted Gini impurity, until min_samples_leaf or max_depth stops it
  4  predict_proba <- mean over trees of each leaf's class-weighted class fractions
  5  feature_importances_ <- mean impurity decrease per feature; read by the rf_importance ranker of ALG-06
  6  calibrated inside the ensemble: false
