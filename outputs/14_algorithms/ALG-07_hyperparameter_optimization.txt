Algorithm ALG-07: Hyperparameter optimization
PV-MEPCG / PulseVision  |  T98.4  |  objective O5

Purpose: Nested Bayesian search: each outer fold's hyperparameters are chosen on inner splits of its own training rows, and the outer test rows are never scored.
Input:   X, y, groups, one model's declared search space, the outer fold map
Output:  per outer fold: the chosen point, the trial log, and one outer score

Algorithm
   1  for each outer fold o:
   2      splits <- 3 grouped, stratified inner splits of o's training rows
   3      budget <- 12 trials per model (exceptions: M5 5); a wall-clock ceiling, if set, is checked between trials
   4      surrogate <- Gaussian process, acquisition EI, initial random points max(3, min(10, self.budget.max_trials // 4)), seed 42
   5      while the budget allows another trial:
   6          theta <- decode(ask()); repair it into the legal space; a violated constraint is logged as an invalid trial
   7          for each inner split (T, V): fit a fresh pipeline of model(theta) on T, predict V, record T and V in the row ledger
   8          score <- mean over inner splits of the objective (balanced_accuracy for binary, macro_f1 for multiclass); a fit that raises is a failed trial
   9          tell(theta, -score); a failed trial is told the worst attainable value
  10      theta* <- the best trial; ties to the earlier trial
  11      assert the ledger holds no row of o's test fold
  12      fit model(theta*) on all of o's training rows; score o's test rows once

Notes
  For the ensembles M6 and M7 the members are what is searched: they declare no hyperparameters of their own.
