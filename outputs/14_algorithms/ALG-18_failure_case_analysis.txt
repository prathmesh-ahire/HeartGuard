Algorithm ALG-18: Failure case analysis
PV-MEPCG / PulseVision  |  T99.4  |  objective O1, O4

Purpose: Back from averages to records: every prediction of the final model is labelled TP, TN, FP or FN, placed in context, and collapsed to how often each record is wrong.
Input:   stored predictions of EXP-A2 M1 (in-domain), EXP-D1 M1 (external); per-record audit context
Output:  per-record failure table, per-category error rates, confused pairs, examples

Algorithm
   1  context <- duration band, noise flag, subset and diagnosis, each loaded from the artifact that owns it
   2  for each source in EXP-A2 M1 (in-domain), EXP-D1 M1 (external):
   3      label every prediction with one of TN, FP, FN, TP; keep the correct ones as denominators
   4      confidence <- probability of the PREDICTED class, np.where( predicted == 1, frame["proba_1"].to_numpy(dtype=float), 1.0 - frame["proba_1"] ); band into 0.50-0.60, 0.60-0.70, 0.70-0.80, 0.80-0.90, 0.90-0.95, 0.95-1.00
   5      join the context; a prediction with no context is an error
   6  per record: n_wrong of n_evaluations; consistently_wrong when every evaluation erred
   7  for each category in duration_band, noise_flag, subset, confidence_band: error rate, FN and FP rates with their denominators; flag groups whose class denominators are under 30 records
   8  multiclass: every off-diagonal confusion cell of EXP-B1, EXP-B1-defaults, EXP-B2, EXP-B2-defaults, EXP-C1-three_class, EXP-C1-two_class, EXP-C2, as a share of its true class
   9  examples <- per (source, error type), the 5 most confident consistently-wrong records (all wrong records when too few)
  10  write T27, G35 and the report, rates always beside their denominators

Notes
  The in-domain and external sources are analysed side by side and never pooled.
