Algorithm ALG-06: Search based feature selection
PV-MEPCG / PulseVision  |  T98.3  |  objective O5

Purpose: SO-04: rank features inside inner training folds, cut every ranking at every k, and choose the cheapest configuration statistically indistinguishable from the best.
Input:   X, y, subject groups, outer folds of repeat(s) 0
Output:  the chosen (ranker, k), per-fold subsets, the consensus subset FE-12, and the all-features-versus-subset comparison

Algorithm
   1  for each outer fold o:
   2      inner <- 3 grouped, stratified splits of o's training rows
   3      for each inner split (T, V) and each ranker r in mutual_info, anova_f, rf_importance, gb_importance:
   4          rank <- r fitted on median-imputed X[T], y[T]; ties broken by column position
   5          for each k in 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 100, 120, 138: S <- top k; fit M4 on X[T, S] in the fold-safe pipeline; score V
   6              J <- alpha (1 - macroF1) + beta k / N + gamma t(S), alpha = 0.7, beta = 0.2, gamma = 0.1, N = 138; t(S) is the extraction time of the families S still needs, normalised by all features
   7  for each (r, k): mean J, mean macro-F1 and its standard error over all inner evaluations
   8  guard <- best mean macro-F1 - 1 x its standard error
   9  (r*, k*) <- lowest mean J among configurations with mean macro-F1 >= guard; ties to the smaller k, then the ranker name
  10  for each outer fold: re-rank with r* on that fold's training rows; keep the top k*
  11  FE-12 <- the k* features chosen in the most folds; ties by column position
  12  score all features and the per-fold subsets on the same outer test folds

Notes
  The guard is a selection rule fixed before the result; it is never tuned to a score.
