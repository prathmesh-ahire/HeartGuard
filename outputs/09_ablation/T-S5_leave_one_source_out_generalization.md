> **The mean over six folds hides a great deal.** training-e alone is 2,141 of the 3,240 records and is 8.5% abnormal; training-c is 31 records and 77.4% abnormal. Holding out training-e leaves a training set with a completely different class balance, and holding out training-c gives a 31-record test fold. The per-fold table is the deliverable.

> A drop here is evidence that the model depends on the acquisition signature. It is NOT proof: holding out a collection also changes the class balance and the patient population, the same confounds EXP-D1 documents. The gap between a model and the source-only baseline on the same fold is the part neither explains.
