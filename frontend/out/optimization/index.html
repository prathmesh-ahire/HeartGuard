Search Optimization

Seven search runs sit between the raw feature matrix and the deployed model: two over hyperparameters, two over feature masks, one sweep over subset size, one over ensemble weights, and one multi-objective front.

Every search runs inside the training fold. The outer test fold is never seen by a search, never scored during one, and never used to fit a scaler or a selector. That is why each run reports a curve per outer fold rather than one curve.
