Recording level and patient level

One subject at every auscultation location, combined into a patient-level murmur result by each declared rule.

CirCor labels the SUBJECT, not the recording, and screens at four auscultation locations. A patient-level indication is therefore a collapse over several recordings, and the rule used to collapse them changes the answer -- so all three declared rules are shown rather than one being chosen silently.
