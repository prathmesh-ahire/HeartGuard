**Notes:** **Optimizing ensemble weights buys almost nothing.** Over 25 folds the grid beats equal weights by **0.0004** balanced accuracy, and **21 of 25 folds choose exactly equal weights**. SLSQP on the objective **never moved on any fold** — balanced accuracy is a step function of the weights, now measured rather than asserted. A defect was found and fixed in Phase 50's M7: its 0.05 simplex lattice cannot represent equal weights (1/3 is not a multiple of any decimal resolution), so its one-standard-error rule could never reach the baseline it shrinks toward and landed 0.00045 *below* it. SO-06 places the shipped 20-feature subset **on** the Pareto front, selected by **90.9% of all (alpha, beta, gamma) weightings** under the guard — and records that gamma is inert, since all 660 sweep evaluations keep a time feature. Full reasoning in `Docs/note.md`.
